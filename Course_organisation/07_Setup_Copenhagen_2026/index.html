    Setup for Copenhagen LUMI courses in 2026¶

    We followed the approach "Dual computers with OBS", for the intro course with broadcast via Zoom and for the PAOW workshop only with recording.

    Packing list¶

    • Separate:
      • Laptop Apple
      • Dual screen MinisForum
      • ASUS screen HDMI
    • Bag for plane
      • Microphones DJI 2
      • Logitech Presenter
      • Logitech Pebble mini-mouse
      • JBL speaker red
    • Power + general bag
      • Bag with power supply
        • UGreen 200W supply + cable
        • USB-C-to-Magsafe cable to power laptop
        • USB-C-to-Lightning cable to power iPad
        • Spare USB-C cable
        • Mini-tripod for microphone receiver
        • Mini-handle for microphone
        • Satechi hub with 3 USB-C ports and ethernet port
      • Cable bag for monitors. Minimal contents:
        • USB-C cable for MinisForum monitor
        • USB-C cable for ASUS monitor
        • micro-HDMI-to-HDMI cable for ASUS monitor
      • General bag with cables, ...
      • Small extension cable with block and built-in USB-A power ports.
    • Bag for video
      • Bag with Elgato HDX60
        • Elgato HDX60
        • Short USB-C-to-USB-C cable
        • Short HDMI cable
      • Box with HDMI 1-to-4 switch
        • HDMI switch
        • Power supply for HDMI switch
        • Short HDMI cable
      • Long HDM-cable (5m or 6m)
    • Extras bag for optional hardware
      • Tonor conference microphone
      • 5m USB-C-to-USB-C cable to connect conference microphone
      • Logitech StreamCam mounted on tripod plate
      • Lightweight red tripod LeoPhoto
      • USB-C-male-to-USB-C-female cable 5m to connect the StreamCam if it is far away from the laptop.
